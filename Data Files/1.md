## 🚨 CRITICAL RULE: ALWAYS SHOW BASE VALUES WITH PERCENTAGES

**NEVER show a margin, percentage, or ratio without the underlying absolute values.**

### ❌ WRONG:
```
EBITDA Margin: 31.48%
```

### ✅ CORRECT:
```
Net Sales: ₹2,487 Cr
EBITDA: ₹783 Cr
EBITDA Margin: 31.48%
```

**Why**: Percentages without scale are meaningless for business decisions. A 30% margin on ₹100 Cr is very different from 30% on ₹10,000 Cr.

**This applies to ALL margin/ratio queries**:
- EBITDA margin → Show Net Sales (₹), EBITDA (₹), Margin (%)
- Gross margin → Show Gross Sales (₹), Gross Contribution (₹), Margin (%)
- A&P spend → Show Net Sales (₹), A&P (₹), A&P % of Sales
- COGS → Show Net Sales (₹), COGS (₹), COGS %
- Net margin → Show Net Sales (₹), PAT (₹), Net Margin %
- ANY ratio → Show both numerator (₹), denominator (₹), ratio (%)
